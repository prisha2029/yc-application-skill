# Team video (MP4)

## What a16z says
- **Optional, but new this cycle. The team says a video almost always helps** [SR-READ].
- What they want: founders talking earnestly about what they're building. They use it to judge storytelling and communication. Production quality isn't scored (Kenan Saleh, Seeam Noor, Troy Kirwin) [SR-ASK].
- **No AI-generated voice** [SR-FAQ].
- **No official length limit is published.** Aim for about 60–90 seconds. That figure is not a16z guidance. It borrows from YC's 1-minute norm [see the yc-application skill], and Emily Bennett's "catch me in the first two" minutes in interviews [SR-ASK] suggests front-loading.
- The video may also be requested after your application advances [SR-READ; SR-71Q]. If they ask for one, send it fast.
- No verified accepted speedrun founder has published a description of their video (0 of 4 accounts).

## The shape (~75 seconds, all founders on camera)
| Time | Beat | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Team | Each founder: name, role, and the single strongest proof of what they've built or done. The team matters most at speedrun. |
| 0:15–0:25 | What | The one-liner: what you build and for whom. |
| 0:25–0:45 | Why us | How long you've known each other and what you've done together, then why this team wins this market. |
| 0:45–1:05 | Earned secret + proof | How you found the idea and the non-obvious thing you know, then your strongest traction or validation number. |
| 1:05–1:15 | Next | What you'll ship and prove in the next 12 weeks. |

Rules:
- Use talking points, not a script.
- Be earnest, not polished. Speak like you'd talk to a smart friend.
- No promo editing, no music, no AI voice.
- Every founder speaks.
- Founders apart? Record a video call.
- MP4 format. Check that it plays before uploading.

## How Claude helps
Write per-founder talking-point cards with a time budget for each card, then add the checklist above. Never write a word-for-word script. Never write lines the founder couldn't say naturally or back up.
