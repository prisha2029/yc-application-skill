# The speedrun form, field by field

The live form is at https://speedrun.a16z.com/apply/form. Field names and character limits below were confirmed there on 2026-11-01. Re-check before submitting, because a16z changes the form between cohorts. Source codes refer to `sources.md`.

**Key facts about how they read it:**
- **The team section matters most.** About 80% of reviewers' time goes on the team and bio sections [SR-ASK; SR-FAQ].
- **Every application is read by humans.** At least two people read each one, and no AI screening is used [SR-71Q].
- **No edits after submitting.** Email updates to sr-team@a16z.com instead [SR-FAQ].

---

## Step 1 — Team

### Full-time / part-time; number of full-time founders; total FTEs
Answer factually. A16z counts anyone with more than 10% equity as a founder [SR-FORM]. Part-time applicants do get in: about a dozen of SR006's ~70 companies hadn't quit their jobs [SR-CHEN-Q]. Give a specific date for going full-time.

### CEO details: country, city, citizenship, education, years of experience
Answer factually. 30 of SR006's ~70 companies used a16z's help with visas [SR-007WRAP].

### Relevant experience — 1,000 characters (per founder)
- **What they check:** this is the most-read field. They want track record, domain expertise and past wins [SR-FORM; SR-ASK].
- **How to answer:**
  - Start with the strongest, most specific win: something you built, shipped, scaled or sold, with a number or outcome.
  - Then the domain expertise that makes you credible for this problem.
  - Then one more proof point.
- **Don't be modest** (Kirwin, Noor). Have someone who knows you read it and tell you what you left out [SR-ASK].
- **Avoid:** long-winded, vague team descriptions. Lead with concrete numbers (Chen) [SR-CHEN-Q].
- **Claude:** use the strongest facts available. Never inflate them. Ask "what's the hardest thing you've built or achieved, and what number shows it?"

### Technical enough to build end-to-end? (Yes/No)
They want a technical cofounder or equivalent expertise [SR-FAQ]. Answer honestly. If the answer is No, use the team-notes field to say who builds the product.

### LinkedIn (required), GitHub / X / portfolio (optional)
- **LinkedIn must be current.** Giving only an X profile was flagged as a mistake [SR-005REV; SR-WHAT].
- Public writing and building in public help [SR-WHAT].
- Make sure every link opens without a login or an access request [SR-005REV].

### Tell us more about the team — 1,000 characters
- **What they check:**
  - How long the founders have known each other (shared history is a strong signal) [SR-WHAT]
  - Why this team wins
  - Complementary roles
  - Other team members and key advisors [SR-FORM]
- **How to answer:**
  - Sentence 1: how long you've known each other, and what you've built or done together.
  - Sentence 2: who owns what (product, engineering, sales).
  - Sentence 3: why this combination wins in this market.
  - Then team members and advisors who matter.
- **Avoid:** too much market theory and too little on why the team is great together (Josh Lu, in TechCrunch) [SR-TC].
- **Cofounder status must be unambiguous** [SR-005REV]. Teams whose founders met only recently are a known risk: two recent breakups involved founders who had only just met [SR-WHAT]. If yours is a new partnership, say how you've tested it, such as a project shipped together.

## Step 2 — Startup

### One-liner — 100 characters
- What you do and for whom [SR-FORM].
- Plain words, with the bottom line first [SR-WHAT].
- **Claude:** write 5 variants of different shapes (category, customer + outcome, verb-first, problem-led, "X for Y" if X is famous). Count characters for each. Recommend one.

### Description — 800 characters
- **What they check:** the problem and what you're building [SR-FORM], plus your insight [SR-WHAT].
- **Structure:** lead with the bottom line (Minto/SCQA) [SR-WHAT].
  1. What you're building and who it's for.
  2. The problem, with one concrete fact.
  3. Your earned secret: how you came to the idea and what non-obvious thing you know [SR-WHAT; SR-READ].
  4. How you're different from the strongest alternative.
- **Avoid:**
  - a demo or product description with no problem statement
  - an inflated TAM
  - unclear differentiation in a crowded category [SR-WHAT]
  - claiming product-market fit with about 100 users [SR-005REV]
  - leaning on "AI" as the whole "why now" (a SR008 roundtable topic, verified only via a summary) [SR-X]

### Category (primary / secondary); where you'll build; month/year founded; website
Answer factually. Speedrun accepts nearly any sector [SR-FAQ]. The program is in person in San Francisco, and both cofounders need to be there (Chen) [SR-CHEN-Q; SR-FAQ].

### Anything else — 1,000 characters (optional)
- **Use it; don't leave it blank.** This is where to put strong news that would otherwise be buried, such as design partners, ARR or a notable customer [SR-WHAT].
- Also good here: a short plan of priorities, metrics and next hires, since a missing plan is a listed mistake [SR-WHAT].
- If you've applied before, add what has changed since.

## Step 3 — Additional

### Traction (optional; fill it whenever you have anything)
- Traction can be qualitative, such as validation conversations. Kenan Saleh calls this the team's learning "slope" [SR-ASK]. If you have numbers, include them.
- **How they rank traction** [SR-WHAT]:
  1. Recurring revenue from significant companies
  2. Short-term contracts
  3. Design partners and customer conversations
  4. Influencer endorsements count least
- Josh Lu's SR005 ranking: traction, then team, then market size [SR-005REV].
- Give exact numbers with periods, label them monthly or weekly, and say what's paid versus free. **Never inflate retention.** Inflated retention numbers were flagged [SR-005REV].

### Funding history / active round
Answer factually. Raising money, or having been through another accelerator, doesn't disqualify you [SR-FAQ].

### Pitch deck — PDF (optional, recommended)
- 6–10 slides with large fonts [SR-WHAT]. Josh Lu says about 7 is enough, and Marcus Segal reads at most 15 [SR-005REV].
- **Include:**
  - your earned secret
  - traction charts
  - product visuals
  - a competitive map that is deeper than a feature checklist
  - a team slide [SR-READ; SR-WHAT]
- Upload a PDF. Never send a link that requires an access request [SR-005REV].
- If the founder has a deck, Claude reviews it against this list. Claude can draft slide-by-slide content but does not design the deck.

### Team video — MP4 (optional; do it)
See `video.md`. The team says a video almost always helps [SR-READ].

### How did you hear about speedrun? (required) / referral
One honest line.
