# Program facts and special situations

As of 2026-11-01. Deadlines, dates and terms change, so confirm at https://speedrun.a16z.com/apply and /faq.

- **SR007** (the latest completed cohort): Jul 27 – Oct 11, 2026. 30,000+ teams applied and about 80 were selected [SR-007KICK; SR-007APP].

## Format
- 12 weeks, in person, at a16z's San Francisco office in North Beach.
- Required sessions: the retreat, Progress Day, First Look Demo Day and Demo Day. Otherwise about one in-person day a week, with full-time work on the startup.
- Housing is not included. Remote participation is not the default [SR-FAQ].
- Andrew Chen says both cofounders must be in San Francisco [SR-CHEN-Q].

## The deal
Up to **$1M** total [SR-DEAL; SR-FAQ]:
- $500K for 10% up front on a SAFE.
- Up to $500K more in your next qualifying round ($2M+) within 18 months.
- Pro rata rights, an option pool, no board seat.
- Terms are generally not negotiable. Speedrun usually doesn't lead seed rounds.
- $10M+ in partner credits, unlocked after the money is wired.

The SAFE cap and whether it's pre- or post-money are **not published**. Claude never states them; send the founder to https://speedrun.a16z.com/the-deal and a lawyer.

Compared with YC, per TechCrunch: YC is $125K for 7% plus $375K on an uncapped MFN SAFE [SR-TC]. Neither a16z nor accepted founders compare the two selection processes. One speedrun founder (SUN) called speedrun a more boutique, high-touch YC, but that was about the program, not selection.

## Eligibility
- Pre-seed or seed: an idea, prototype or early product, with a team already building. Traction is not required.
- You can apply before incorporating, after raising, or after another accelerator [SR-FAQ].
- You need a technical cofounder or equivalent expertise [SR-FAQ].
- Founders must be 18+ [SR-READ]. Founders have come from 20+ countries, and there's help with visas [SR-FAQ; SR-007WRAP].
- **Students and recent grads** may fit a16z's Alpha Fellowship (up to $250K) better [SR-FAQ].
- **Sectors:** nearly any. The team matters more than the category [SR-FAQ].

## Solo founders
Accepted, but a16z prefers multi-founder teams with complementary skills [SR-READ]. In the team field, explain who builds the product and which advisors or early hires fill the gaps.

## Part-time founders
About a dozen of SR006's ~70 companies hadn't quit their jobs when they applied [SR-CHEN-Q]. Give a specific date for going full-time.

## Pre-product / idea stage
Accepted. Sitch (Cohort 004) applied with a deck and a vision, and SUN (Cohort 006) with no product or traction. Lean on the team's track record, the earned secret, and qualitative traction: customer conversations, design partners, learning slope [SR-ASK; SR-WHAT].

## Reapplying
About 30% of SR005 teams included a founder who had been rejected before [SR-REJECT]. You get no individual feedback, and the same team isn't re-reviewed within one window [SR-FAQ]. On reapplying, say in "Anything else" what changed: new traction, the founder now full-time, a cofounder added, sharper insight.

## Deferral
Possible. Sitch was accepted into SR003 and deferred to SR004 to rebuild its product (see the cases file).

## After submitting
You can't edit the application. Email material updates (new revenue, a key hire, a funding round) to sr-team@a16z.com [SR-FAQ].

## Scams / shortcuts
No warm intro is needed. SUN applied cold. Ignore paid services that promise admission.
