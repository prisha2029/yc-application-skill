# Special situations: idea-stage, solo, reapplying, late, non-US, equity

Facts below are as of the sources' dates. For anything date-sensitive (deadlines, the deal, batches), check https://www.ycombinator.com/faq and https://www.ycombinator.com/apply. YC changes these.

## Batches and deadlines
- YC runs **four batches a year**: Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall. The 2026 Demo Days were in March, June, September and December [YC-DEMODAYS].
- **Late applications** keep being reviewed, and YC funds many late applicants each batch. A late application is never treated as on time, so apply as soon as possible [YC-FAQ].
- **Early Decision:** you can apply now for a later batch. If accepted, you're funded immediately and your spot is held [YC-ED].
- After submitting, you can't keep editing. You can send an update for material changes, such as a co-founder joining or leaving, or a big new milestone [YC-FAQ].

## Idea-stage
- About **40% of each batch is just an idea**, and most companies have no revenue. Apply when you have a team and an idea you're excited about [YC-FAQ]. "There is no such thing as too early" [DC-2018]; Jared Friedman: there's nothing to lose by applying [JF-TIMING].
- How to write it: put the weight on the founders and the insight. Use the problem → solution → insight framing [KH-2019]. Show proof of the problem, such as user conversations, your own experience or a waitlist. Say plainly what exists today [DC-2023].

## Solo founders
- YC accepts solo founders regularly but says it's harder, and a co-founder improves your odds. It points to Co-founder Matching [YC-FAQ]. 8.5% of S16 was solo [YC-MISC].
- Don't add a co-founder you barely know just to have one [YC-FFC2016].
- Harj Taggar: in about 90% of cases, put your effort into finding a co-founder first. The exception is high conviction, domain experience, and the ability to build v1 alone [HT-COF].
- In the application, show you can build the product yourself and explain why you're solo. Vitable Health S20 was rejected solo in 2019 after a recruited co-founder quit. It was accepted solo in 2020 with strong growth.
- No technical founder plus nothing launched is the lowest-odds combination. The burden of proof is on you [DC-2023].

## Reapplying
- About **half of each batch applied more than once**. Progress since your last application is a strong signal. Recommendations carry over [YC-FAQ]. 45% of S16 had been rejected before [YC-MISC].
- What changed for 10 verified rejected-then-accepted founders, in order of frequency:
  1. **New traction:** paying users, MRR, usage, GitHub stars.
  2. **A direct answer to YC's stated objection** (Metorial F25, InsForge Spring 2026).
  3. **Founders going full-time**, or fixing a co-founder gap (Metorial, webapp.io, Vitable).
  4. **A sharper one-liner and a shorter application** (Metorial, Lingo.dev).
  5. **Adding a demo video** (Lingo.dev, webapp.io).
  6. **More user interviews** (InsForge, webapp.io).
  7. **Alumni reviewing the draft and running mocks** (Lingo.dev).
- Claude should ask for the previous rejection reason and make sure the new application answers it explicitly, ideally in the progress or idea answers.

## Students and people with jobs
You can apply while studying or employed, but you must go full-time if accepted [YC-FAQ]. Expect "how much time can you commit?" in the interview (Basedash S20, Nebullam W19).

## Non-US founders
- You don't need to incorporate before applying. YC invests in US, Canadian, Cayman and Singapore entities. Companies elsewhere set up a parent company there. Visas and incorporation are handled after acceptance [YC-FAQ].
- Non-US founders accepted include Metorial (Austria, F25), Nuvocargo (Ecuador, W18), and Indian founders at Stilt (W16) and SuperKalam (W23).

## Equity and legal questions
- Answer these factually and completely: incorporation status, founders' equity split, other investors, IP/noncompete issues, and any code written by non-founders.
- Investors check whether the equity split is roughly equal [MS-PITCH]. Harj Taggar defaults to equal splits [HT-COF]. If yours is very unequal, give the reason in one plain sentence.
- YC's standard deal, as of the source date: $500k total. That is $125k for 7% on a post-money SAFE, plus $375k on an uncapped MFN SAFE [YC-DEAL]. Claude should never make up legal or financial terms. Point to https://www.ycombinator.com/deal and suggest a lawyer for real legal questions.

## Raising money now / already funded
You can apply while raising; say so. Prior funding and prior accelerators are fine [YC-FAQ].

## Scams
No introduction is needed (54.7% of S16 had no recommendation [YC-MISC]). Paid services promising admission are, per Dalton Caldwell, scammers [DC-2023].
