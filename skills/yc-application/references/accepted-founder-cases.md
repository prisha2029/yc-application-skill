# Accepted-founder evidence base

**Verification rule.** A founder's account is used only if their company has a page in YC's company directory (ycombinator.com/companies/…). The batch comes from that page where it showed one. Otherwise it comes from a Launch HN / Show HN post tagged with YC, or the founder's own post, and that is noted. Coaching services, anonymous posts and unverifiable claims were excluded. Researched 2026-11-01.

All content below is paraphrased. Read the sources for the full context.

## Patterns, with how many cases support each
Counts are out of the 12 written applications, unless stated otherwise.
- Specific numbers in progress answers, even tiny ones: **11/12**
- Idea from the founders' own problem: **10/12**
- Short, plain one-liner (X-for-Y or category): **8 of 9** with a one-liner
- Competitors named and a feared one admitted: **8/12**
- Applied and been rejected before: **9/12**. Across all verified accounts: 10 rejected-then-accepted.
- Evidence of paying before the product was finished: **5/12**
- Solo founders accepted with traction or a track record: **5/12**
- Bottom-up market sizing used or wished for: **3/12**. The most common self-critique.
- Mock interviews before the interview: **9/19** accounts
- Follow-up or second interview: **5/19** accounts

## Gaps
- Few detailed founder-video accounts were found (6).
- Indian founder accounts with substantive detail are limited to Stilt (W16) and SuperKalam (W23).
- Bio and hardware are not represented.
- X, LinkedIn and some Medium pages blocked automated reading.
